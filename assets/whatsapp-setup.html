STEP 6

Enable and check the connection

  1. Wait for both templates to be approved and configure any payment/billing requirements shown by Meta.
  2. Set WHATSAPP_ENABLED=true in Railway, then deploy the variables.
  3. Open Dashboard → Notifications → Check account connection. It should show the store sender as verified.
  4. Use a signed-in customer who has enabled order updates. A normal confirmed purchase queues its order bill; online purchases wait for verified payment.
  5. To test the cart reminder, enable the separate optional reminder choice, add products, and stop interacting for 20 minutes. Expect one message for the complete bag, with one link into checkout.

The ₹1 catalogue check and ₹10 owner gateway check do not create delivery orders or send order notifications. Use a regular purchase when testing order messages.

The worker checks newly queued messages every few seconds. Actual message delivery depends on Meta. Dashboard status distinguishes queued, accepted, delivered and failed sends.

How the saved choices work

Customers agree once. Their order-update permission and optional cart-reminder permission are saved in MySQL and are not requested at every checkout. Account settings and STOP can turn them off.

Cart links belong to the signed-in customer, restore the whole saved bag and open checkout. They do not charge a bank account automatically: the customer reviews the current bill and approves payment in Razorpay. Long messages show a product summary with a clear “more items” note; the linked checkout contains the full bill.
